## 📝 Question

At a family function, there are 40 members. If every member shakes hands with every other member exactly once, how many handshakes are possible?

## ✅ Answer

780 handshakes.

## 🧠 Concept

Each handshake is between two different people.

We must avoid counting the same handshake twice.

For example, A shaking hands with B is the same as B shaking hands with A.

Therefore, we use the combination formula:

\[
\binom{n}{2}=\frac{n(n-1)}{2}
\]

## 💻 Solution

Given:

- Total members = 40

Formula:

\[
\text{Handshakes}=\frac{n(n-1)}{2}
\]

Substituting:

\[
=\frac{40(40-1)}{2}
\]

\[
=\frac{40\times39}{2}
\]

\[
=20\times39
\]

\[
\boxed{780}
\]

## 🎯 Shortcut

For handshake problems:

**n × (n − 1) ÷ 2**

## 🌍 Applications

This formula can also be used for:

- Matches between teams where every team plays every other team once.
- Counting unique pairs.
- Connections between people.
- Pairing problems.
- Network connections between nodes.
