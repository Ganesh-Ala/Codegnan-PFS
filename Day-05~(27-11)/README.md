### Important Concepts

**HCF:** Highest Common Factor.

The largest number that divides all given numbers exactly.

**LCM:** Lowest Common Multiple.

The smallest number divisible by all given numbers.

### Prime Factorization Rules

- HCF: Select the lowest powers of common prime factors.
- LCM: Select the highest powers of all prime factors.

### Important Formula

For two numbers:

HCF × LCM = First Number × Second Number

### Examples

| Numbers | HCF | LCM |
|---------|-----|-----|
| 12, 8, 6 | 2 | 24 |
| 16, 24, 48 | 8 | 48 |
| 25, 45, 30 | 5 | 450 |
| 36, 24, 18 | 6 | 72 |

### Word Problem

Given:

- HCF = 11
- LCM = 7700
- One number = 275

Other number:

(11 × 7700) ÷ 275 = 308

**Answer: 308**
